About the Library

Academic Knowledge Repository

The proposed Agronomy Library is designed to organise academic books, postgraduate and doctoral theses, journals, research papers and other scholarly materials related to agricultural sciences.

Knowledge Accessibility

The platform aims to improve the discovery and accessibility of authorised academic resources for students, researchers, faculty members and the wider agricultural community.
